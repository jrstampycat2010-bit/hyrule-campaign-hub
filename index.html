Campaign Spine — Five Acts

This is the structure that was just written into the app. It replaces the old six-chapter BotW retread.

Act I opens at Wetland Stable (Horn at the Ridge). Act II is the Master Sword. Act III is provinces + Chamber of Courage. Act IV is the Fierce Deity Mask choice. Act V is the fused Demon King.

Act 1 · Central Hyrule / Wetland · Level 1–2

Approach

Roads open. The party arrives at Wetland Stable, takes Emri's Red-camp bargain, finds a Piece of Heart in the pot, and is pointed toward the Temple of Time rebuild and a secret room on the Plateau.

Hook: Rain on the stable roof. Emri has a job that pays in information and a warm bowl. A Red camp is too close. The pot has something that is not stew.

1. Wetland Stable

The stable is wet wood and warm light. Horses shift. A note sits lower on the board than the others and does not thin. Emri wipes a chip on the counter and does not look up until you speak.

Emri offers the Red-camp bargain. The air has not yielded. Extra bowl rim-facing the door. Preferred hook: Temple of Time + secret room. No Sheikah runner.

2. The Red Camp

Red Bokoblins. A cooking pot. A lookout that is not quite asleep.

Clear or scatter. Salvage after. Emri considers the bargain kept if the camp is no longer a problem.

3. The Pot

Back at the stable the pot is still going. Something solid sits under the surface that is not potato.

Piece of Heart (1 Heart = 4 HP). Emri points to the Plateau when asked.

4. Road to the Plateau

Act I closes when the party has the Temple of Time hook and a reason to keep moving.

DM: Prefer Temple of Time hook. ~2 hours. Fail-forward if the camp goes badly. Sensory stays grounded.

Act 2 · Central Hyrule · Level 2–4

The Blade

First Light of the Master Sword. Tempering begins. The Correcting Cut is learned the hard way. Latent power is deliberately last.

1. First Light

The pedestal is older than the ruin around it. When the right hand closes, the sword does not flare — it settles.

Master Sword drawn. First Light active.

2. Tempering Road

Full Tempering can be attempted. Failure is fail-forward: pick up where you left off. Nothing is stripped. Full party may help.

3. True + Correcting Cut

There is a cut that finishes cleanly and a cut that finishes the right thing. The sword notices the difference.

Use latest Master Sword Improved rules (v91+). Latent power is the Act III→IV threshold material.

Act 3 · All provinces · Level 4–8

Provinces + Upgrades

Domain arcs, stables, workbenches, color-tier upgrades, Chamber of Courage. Apex-ready = Stage 2 + chamber and/or upgrades. Latent power proven here or at the Mask threshold.

1. Domain Arcs

Long-middle atlas material. Color tiers (Red/Blue/Silver/Gold) and salvage drive growth. Drop-in volumes support; they do not award Apex.

2. Chamber of Courage

A place that does not care about your title. It cares whether the blade and the hand are ready to be tested together.

Apex proving (Trial of the Triforce). Full party may help.

3. Threshold

Act III ends when the party is Apex-ready or standing where the Mask becomes the next necessary question.

Act 4 · Threshold · Level 8–12

The Mask

Fierce Deity Mask is offered. Absorb locks Fierce Form + absorbed path (no voice, three silent tells). Keep leaves the man speaking under ordinary rules.

1. The Offering

The Mask is empty when it is not on a face. The man who once wore what it remembers does not want the title. He will answer to Link if the room is not ending.

Run with the Mask absorb companion (latest Improved one-page run-order).

2. Absorb or Keep

Absorb = Fierce Form + silent path. Keep = living corrections, unpaid things, limited intrusions. Choice is permanent texture, not a power-up screen.

3. After the Choice

Play the consequences. No bond step 4. Blood Moon makes him quieter, not stronger.

Use Hyrule_Fierce_Deity.pdf + latest Mask Absorb Improved companion.

Act 5 · Castle / final ground · Level 12–20+

The Cycle

Fused Demon King / Demise. Three phases. Legendary + lair + optional mythic. Master Sword and Fierce Deity kill conditions apply.

1. Approach the Wound

Pink fire that is not fire. A throne that has been occupied too long.

2. The Fuse

Scores climb toward hard cap 60. Full fusion: AC 32, 1000 HP (250 Hearts), extreme DCs. Use the Demon King handout.

3. Kill Conditions

Master Sword latent truth + Fierce Deity presence (or absence). The ending should feel like five acts of decisions, not one lucky roll.

Fourfold Apex (Master Sword + Four Sword) is removed by DM ruling.

Core Rules (aligned)

Hearts — 1 Heart = 4 HP. Pieces of Heart track progress.

Color Tiers — Red / Blue / Silver / Gold for gear and enemies.

Salvage & Workbenches — Upgrade loop through the provinces.

Master Sword — Latent last; fail-forward on Full Tempering; party may help in chambers.

Fierce Deity — Absorb vs Keep; absorbed path has no voice.
