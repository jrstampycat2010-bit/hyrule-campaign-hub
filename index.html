Campaign Structure — Five Acts

Act I — Approach

Roads open. Wetland Stable, Emri’s bargain, Red camp, Piece of Heart in the pot. Preferred hook: Temple of Time rebuild + secret room on the Plateau. No Sheikah runner.

Act II — The Blade

First Light → True + Correcting Cut. Master Sword milestones begin. Tempering and early proving.

Act III — Provinces + Upgrades

Domain arcs, Chamber of Courage = Apex proving (Trial of the Triforce). Apex-ready = Stage 2 + chamber and/or upgrades. Latent power of the Master Sword comes last and is required for true Apex.

Act IV — The Mask

Mid-act Mask sequence. Fierce Deity advises on absorb vs keep. Absorb locks Fierce Form + absorbed path. Keep leaves the man speaking under ordinary rules.

Act V — The Cycle

Final confrontation with the fused Demon King / Demise. Master Sword + Fierce Deity kill conditions apply.

Fourfold Apex (Master Sword + Four Sword fusion) has been removed by DM ruling. The Master Sword does not fuse with the Four Sword.

Core Rules in Use

Hearts — 1 Heart = 4 HP. Pieces of Heart and Pieces of Magic track progress.

Color Tiers — Red / Blue / Silver / Gold for gear and enemies.

Salvage & Workbenches — Core economy and upgrade loop.

Companion Books — Separate materials for DM and players.

Master Sword

Latent power is last and required for true Apex.

Full Tempering failure = pick up where you left off (nothing is stripped).

Full party may help in Full Tempering, Trial of the Sword, and the Chambers. Bearer-locked finals remain Lonk / Eclipse.

Act III → IV threshold is the latent proving point.

Latest refined rules: Hyrule_Master_Sword_Improved_v91.pdf (true 1-page navy/light-gold layout, gold grid tables, fail-forward example, Apex independence table, Fierce Form table, Quick Rules).

Fierce Deity (Link of the Eye)

Half-Hylian, half-Sheikah. DM ancestor. Hates the title. Lost because he took the duel while Ganondorf kept the war. Told the princess’s grandmother the bloodline secret.

Private cast: mother Ien of the Eye, father Calden, aunt Vessa (cut the blank mask), page Pim, weaver Mara.

Want under the want: be called Link in a room that is not ending.

Kept path: 3 intrusions / session. Absorbed path: no voice, three silent tells only.

Latest Mask absorb companion: Hyrule_Fierce_Deity_Mask_Absorb_Improved_v88.pdf (one-page offering run-order table with numbered priority).

Main handout: Hyrule_Fierce_Deity.pdf (living corrections, unpaid things, session strip, etc.).

Peoples & Final Boss

Demon People — Custom homebrew: red scaled skin, transforming tail weapon (dagger → greataxe), 40 ft flight, fire resistance, Blood Moon Surge. Balanced version of player request.

Demon King (fused Ganondorf / Demise) — Level 100 final boss. Ability scores scale from base (STR 22 / DEX 20 / CON 21 / mental 20) to hard cap 60 when fully fused. Full fusion: AC 32, +33 to hit/saves, DC 41/43, 1000 HP (250 Hearts), three phases + legendary + lair + optional mythic. Triforce of Power + Demise’s Eternal Hatred. Master Sword / Fierce Deity kill condition.
